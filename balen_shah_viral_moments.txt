--- THE UNHINGED FACEBOOK POSTS ---

1. "F**k America, F**k India, F**k China, F**k UML, F**k Congress, F**k RSP, F**k RPP, F**k Maobaadi."
   — A late-night 2025 Facebook post that went globally viral. He deleted it. The internet did not forget.

2. "Nothing happened today, but if the government stops any vehicle of our metropolitan city tomorrow,
   I will set Singha Durbar on fire. Remember, you thieves."
   — Posted after traffic police stopped a car carrying his wife for ~2 minutes on a public holiday.
   Possibly the most unhinged thing a sitting mayor has ever posted.

3. "The uproar at Singha Durbar will start from today. If we genuinely want to clean this country,
   we need to dump these leaders in the trash."
   — A Saturday morning Facebook rant.

4. "Dear Prime Minister, America has called us corrupt, India has called us beggars, and China does not
   respect us. Forget that I am a mayor — will you at least give us a basis to live as Nepalis?"
   — Viral open letter to PM Oli.

5. "I would like to request an amendment to the Constitution to give the Supreme Court all the authority
   related to approving building designs and taking action against illegal constructions."
   — Sarcastic Facebook burn after the Supreme Court stopped his bulldozer at Norvic Hospital.


--- THE "I DON'T FOLLOW COURTS" ERA ---

6. "I am not going to comply with any law and court as far as the country's sovereignty and independence
   is concerned."
   — Said while banning an Indian film, defying a court order to allow its screening.

7. "The court and the government are Indian slaves."
   — Said in the same breath as #6. The government of Nepal called his move a stunt.
   He called them colonized.

8. "I am ready to face any punishment for that, but the film will not be screened or allowed to be screened."
   — Regarding the Indian film Adipurush, which suggested Sita was born in India.
   He refused even after the Supreme Court issued orders.

9. When later asked about his Singha Durbar threat before a commission, he DENIED ever posting it —
   even though the screenshot was already all over social media.
   One user replied: "Seems the testimony was also given in rhythm, nephew?"


--- BULLDOZER BOSS ENERGY ---

10. Personally riding alongside bulldozers through Kathmandu Mall, New Baneshwor, Khichapokhari and

11. Dumping Kathmandu's garbage in front of government offices — literally ordering waste to be piled
    outside Singha Durbar — to protest the federal government's failure to help with waste management.

12. Halting all garbage collection from Singha Durbar as a political protest.

13. Giving a 24-hour ultimatum to Norvic International Hospital to demolish its own illegal structures —

14. Demolishing homes and businesses that had official government approval without prior notice or
    compensation — then when critics complained, essentially shrugging.
